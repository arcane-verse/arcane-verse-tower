The Five Gods

Five divine powers govern the Tower. A player may hold one true Vassal contract at a time.

Vassal rules

  • Players may interact with all five Gods, but may have only one true Vassal contract at a time.
  • Vassalhood requires a divine trial, eligibility, and the God's acceptance.
  • Vassals gain a title, a unique perk, God-specific quests, and possible exclusive rewards.
  • Changing allegiance may require breaking the existing contract and may remove its benefits.
  • Gods cannot freely overwrite another player's progression; the Tower remains the governing authority.
